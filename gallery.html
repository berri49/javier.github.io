Mount Daraitan: "Rocky Mountain of Rizal"

Location: Rizal
Height: 739 MASL
Difficulty: 4/9 (Minor hike)

Known for limestone formations, steep rocky sections, and the scenic Tinipak River.

Mascap Trilogy: Mt. Ayaas, Mt. Espadang Bato, and Mt. Sipit Ulang

Location: Rizal
Height: 600-800+ MASL depending on peak
Difficulty: 5/9 (Intermediate)

A network of limestone peaks and ridges in Rodriguez, Rizal, known for scrambling sections and panoramic Sierra Madre views.

Batulao: "Bato sa Ilaw"

Location: Batangas
Height: 811 MASL
Difficulty: 4/9 (Minor hike)

A grassy ridge mountain in Nasugbu with open trails, rolling hills, and sweeping views of Batangas and Cavite.

Tarak Ridge: Mount Mariveles

Location: Bataan
Height: 1,130 MASL
Difficulty: 5/9 (Intermediate)

A forested mountain trail leading to a rocky ridge with views of Manila Bay and the West Philippine Sea.

Mount Makiling: Maria Makiling Herself

Location: Laguna
Height: 1,090 MASL
Difficulty: 5/9 (Intermediate)

A biodiverse dormant volcano covered in dense rainforest, known for mossy trails, hot springs, and rich wildlife.

Mount Ulap: Mountain of Clouds

Location: Benguet
Height: 1,846 MASL
Difficulty: 3/9 (Minor hike)

A scenic Cordillera hike with pine forests, grassland ridges, and expansive mountain views above the clouds.
